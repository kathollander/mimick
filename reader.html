Mimick for the web

Read any PDF out loud in a natural voice, slow or fast, with the words lit as they are spoken — in a browser tab, with nothing installed. Your document is opened by your own browser and never leaves your computer.

What this does

Read aloud in a natural voice
A Piper voice, running in this tab. It is downloaded once, and works offline after that.

Follow along
As Mimick reads, the sentence and the word being said are lit on the page.

Read at your speed
From 0.75× to 4×, changed at any moment.

Highlight and annotate
Four colours, and notes beside the page. They are kept in this browser, and Download a copy gives you the PDF with them in it, as regular PDF annotations other readers can see.

Take it with you
Convert a document, some pages of it or a passage to an MP3.

Keeps your place
Come back to a document and pick up where you left off.

Built on

MuPDF (pages, text and annotations) · Pyodide (Python in the browser) · Piper and espeak-ng (the voice) · ONNX Runtime (running it) · LAME, as lamejs (MP3 files)

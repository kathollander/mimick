Mimick for the web

Read any PDF out loud in a natural voice, slow or fast, with the words lit as they are spoken — in a browser tab, with nothing installed. Your document is opened by your own browser and never leaves your computer.

What this does

Read aloud in a natural voice
A Piper voice, running in this tab. It is downloaded once, and works offline after that.

Follow along
As Mimick reads, the sentence and the word being said are lit on the page.

Read at your speed
From 0.75× to 4×, changed at any moment.

Highlight and annotate
Four colours, and notes beside the page. They are kept in this browser, and Save a copy gives you the PDF with them in it, as regular PDF annotations other readers can see, and Export notes gives you them on their own, as text.

Take it with you
Convert a document, some pages of it or a passage to an MP3.

Keeps your place
Come back to a document and pick up where you left off.

Works offline
Once it has loaded fully, Mimick opens and reads with no internet connection.

How to use it

Open a PDF, Word (.docx), OpenDocument (.odt), EPUB or .txt file with File ▾, Ctrl+O, or by dropping it on the page. Press Space to read and pause — the first time, the voice downloads once (about 60 MB). Change the voice and speed in the top bar; ▶ plays a sample first. Click a sentence to read from there, or select text and press Enter. Ctrl+F finds text. Ctrl+H highlights, Ctrl+M adds a note, Ctrl+S saves a copy with them in it. Every key is under Help ▾ → Keyboard shortcuts.

Privacy

Every document you open stays on this computer: your own browser reads it, and nothing is uploaded. Mimick downloads only the page itself, the first time, and each voice you use, once, from Hugging Face — checked against a fixed hash. Your highlights, notes, reading positions and settings are kept in this browser's storage and nowhere else. No accounts, no analytics, no tracking.

Browsers

Built and tested in Google Chrome, for laptops and desktops. Brave, Edge and other Chromium browsers should work the same. Firefox and Safari are untested: there, an MP3 downloads when it is finished rather than being saved as it is made, and Mimick cannot be installed as an app.

Built on

MuPDF (pages, text and annotations) · Pyodide (Python in the browser) · Piper and espeak-ng (the voice) · ONNX Runtime (running it) · LAME, as lamejs (MP3 files)

Voices

Every voice is a Piper voice, trained on recordings people shared, and their licences ask for these credits. Kathleen and Southern English are built on Ryan, whose licence is non-commercial: they may only be used free of charge, as Mimick is.

# Notes on Listening

This short document is written for Mimick's checks. It is laid out as a word processor would lay it out, with headings, a list and a table, so the reader can be tested on something other than a PDF.

## Why listen

Hearing a text while following it on the page keeps the pace steady. The eyes and the ears hold each other to the line.

- Listening helps when attention drifts.
- A faster voice can hold attention better.

## How to begin

Open the file, press the space bar, and follow the words that are lit.
